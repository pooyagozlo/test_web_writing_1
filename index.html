Finding a Good Place to Study

Students need different kinds of study spaces depending on whether they are working alone, meeting with a group, or attending an online meeting. This guide introduces several study spaces and explains their main features.

Quiet Study Spaces

View Quite Space Recs

Quiet study spaces are useful when students need to read, write, or concentrate without interruption.

Group Study Spaces

Group study spaces allow students to discuss projects and work together without disturbing other students.

What to Look For

  • Available seating
  • Power outlets
  • Reliable Wi-Fi
  • Low noise level

How to Choose a Study Space

  1. Decide whether you need individual or group space
  2. Check the expected noise level
  3. Check whether power outlets are available
  4. Select the space that best fits your needs
